kuch games hain jinmein exercise nahi hoti poori

yeh non physical indoor games hain...

1. Chess ( Halanke iska aage developed form AoE2:TC theek hai, insmein 3D form mein hota hai chess... ) ... Ismein king's type to wahi hai Wooden waali, but aajkal modern mein to glass waali zyada achchi lagti hai...plastic waali to aap mangaate hi rehte for easy refilling etc...ya even magnetic waali bhi hoti hai...
2. Cards ( Ismein develop ho ke candy crush wagairah jaise pattern matching games chal gaye hain, but beware inse aage sugar wagairah ki problem ho sakti hai in case exercise proper nahi karo to...) ... Card games bhi aage bahut saari hoti hain Rummy / Seep / Bhabhi / dukki pe dukki ... inmein se rummy main thodi theek khel paata tha chotte hotey.... Flash paise waala maine standard ek hi baar khela hai check karne ke liye ke galat hai ... jabke pooja poora end tak khelti rehti hai....Jigsaw bhi bachche ko dikhaane tak hi theek hai sirf....badey ho ke to koi khaas faida nahi{iska aage developed form blocks etc. waale hote hain}.... Trump Cards bhi WWF chalti thi tab popular thi... kuch aur bhi nayi nayi card games hoti hain bilkul alag type ke cards waali bhi...
3. Ludo / Snakes and ladders {ismein dice mein problem hai , main to zyadatar haarta hi tha...}
4. Carrom / Checkers { Ismein exercise to hogi nahi zyada , sirf carrom mein ungliyon ki hogi ... ya checkers mein thoda bahut dimaag lagega... iske voters keh rahey hain ke shiv{pandey bhau} ek jagah hota hai...udhar(Ferozpur) jaane ke liye khelna padta hai carrom...} .... Pool/Billiards to bilkul hi thuss hai .... wo to wo game table hi afford karne ke liye aapko club join karna padega.... Maine to aise IIT mein dekha hua tha ... aur Arcade (Nintendo) waale pe khela hua tha Pehle... Actual mein ek photo hai pooja ki wo khelte pose karti huyi himachal mein {Honeymoon ke liye...} ... Pair ke anguthe se khelenge to pair ke anguthey ki exercise hogi... Carrom board bhi alag alag size ke ho sakte hain Bada / Bahut Bada / Chotta / Bahut chotta etc..  aur gitti bhi Blue/White/Red{plastic} ya Black/Brown/Pink{Wooden etc.} waali hoti hain...
5. Table Tennis mein bhi bahut kam hai exercise...
6. Disc game bhi bahut kam hai exercise regard mein{Fun republic mein tha ya aajkal ghar pe hi le aaye hain bachchon ke liye..}...
7. Swimming mere ko nahi aata... par kam depth waali locations ... Rara Sahib / Haridwar waale safe locations pe main bhi dubki le leta.....udhar itna khatra nahi hai doobne ka{in case swimming nahi aati..}...
8. Walking / Cycling ko bhi sirf Bhagat Singh ka vote hone ke kaaran maanana pad raha hai mere ko ... mere according to kam rating hai...par Bhagat Singh keh raha hai uske according yeh theek hain...
9. VR waali game maine kheli nahi kabhi...halanke main khud VR Show mein dikhaa deta even with Rock Show....halanke VR mode mein gaming hi hoti mere ko raat mein sotey time... very very blood-bath battles....
10. Books bhi technical to fir bhi chal jaati / par kuch log novels bhi padhte hain...wo unko khud ko pata hoga kaise padh paate.....e.g. mummy abhigyan-shakuntla wagairah bataate hain jo unhone top-rated padha hua hai....
11. Programming to bahut hi zyada elite hai ... par jo sirf render kar ke hi nikaalega usko to kya hi faayda tha ke programming bhi hoti thi ek....
